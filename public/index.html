01 / THE ATS APPROACH

Addoma Trading Services is a Sudanese engineering and consulting institution with more than two decades of field experience across power engineering, energy systems, and commercial and industrial refrigeration.

We deliver vital projects for private, humanitarian and development partners across Sudan and the Middle East — from UNICEF vaccine cold-chain projects to diesel generation, load-sharing, remote monitoring and AI-enabled predictive maintenance.
